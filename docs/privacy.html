Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What Shortsbot does

Shortsbot is a personal tool that uploads short videos to its owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API. It is used only by its owner.

Data we access

Shortsbot requests one Google permission: youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to the channel of the signed-in account. It does not read, store or share any other Google account data.

Storage

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer or server. Shortsbot keeps a local list of Twitch clip IDs it has already used, to avoid duplicates. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube Terms

By using Shortsbot you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
