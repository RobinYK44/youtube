Shortsbot

▶ Developed with YouTube

Shortsbot is a personal tool that turns popular Twitch clips into vertical YouTube Shorts and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel. The original streamer is always credited by name, with links to their channel and the original clip.

The tool is used only by its owner. It does not offer accounts to other people.

YouTube API Services

Shortsbot uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. By using Shortsbot you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles your data.
